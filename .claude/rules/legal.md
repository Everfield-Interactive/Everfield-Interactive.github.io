# Ownership: legal

These folders, and the notice files (`LICENSE`, the notice in `README.md`, and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`), belong to the legal workstream (Section 14 of `docs/BUILD_GUIDE.md`).

- If this session is the legal workstream, in the `legal` worktree, follow `docs/workstreams/legal.md`. Drafts live in the `legal` folder of `EVERFIELD_SOURCE`, outside the repository.
- If this session is the lead or the effects designer, do not edit these files. Append a notice to `inbox-legal.md` in the `workstreams` folder of `EVERFIELD_SOURCE`.
- Nothing legal is published without Kiefy's approval. Approved texts enter the repository through the lead.
- A draft is not legal advice, and the workstream never invents facts about the studio.
